Your trap line, in your pocket.

This is the real Opossum Foot app, running in your browser on a sample line in Prairie County. Every screen, list, and picker works the way it works on a phone — try it the way you'd run a line.

  1. 1
    Drop a pin where the set is

    Tap the pin button, tap the map, then drag the pin to the exact spot before you save. Up to three sets of your own in the demo.

  2. 2
    Log a catch, check a set

    Open any set on the line and use the app's own Log a Catch and Mark Checked — seasons, counts, and all.

  3. 3
    Work the other tabs

    History, Totals, Seasons, and Route are the app's real screens — seasons for the state, your line on the map.

You're not stuck with the pin where it lands. GPS can be off by a few yards — drag the pin to where the set really is.

That's the way the app works on your own ground, too.

About this demo

  • Sample data only. The sets, catches, and weather are made up — a sample line in Prairie County, out on anonymous ground.
  • Nothing is saved. No account, no sign-in, nothing written to your device. Leave or hit Reset and the sample line is back the way it started.
  • Your own ground. Tap the crosshair and the map flies to you — your location stays in your browser; it is never sent anywhere and never stored. You can make and drag sets there, too.
  • Three sets of your own. The demo lets you add three sets so you can feel the flow. The full app carries your whole line.

The full app runs on iPhone and Android — no account, nothing to sign into, one price once. Free to start: three dozen sets on one line.
